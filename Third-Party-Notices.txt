Supertonic 3
Satori's read-aloud voices use Supertonic 3: Supertone's model weights,
converted to Core ML by FluidInference. They are not part of the app; they are
downloaded on request from Hugging Face and run by the system's Core ML. The
weights are licensed under OpenRAIL-M, which carries use restrictions; read
the license text before relying on them.
https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/supertonic-3-coreml
